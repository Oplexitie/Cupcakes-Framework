TONY - REQUIRED AUDIO
=====================

Sound effects (WAV or OGG recommended):
- tony_scream.ogg     - The disruptive scream (plays when audio disruption triggers)
- tony_tantrum.ogg    - Looping tantrum sound (optional, can reuse scream)
- tony_movement.ogg   - Footstep/movement sound (optional, plays when moving)

Notes:
- Scream should be loud and jarring - it's meant to be annoying!
- Keep files under 1MB each for good performance
- OGG format recommended for smaller file size
