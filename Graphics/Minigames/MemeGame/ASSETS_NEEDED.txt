MEME MINIGAME - REQUIRED ASSETS
================================

DANK MEMES (upvote to survive):
- doge.png           - Classic Doge
- illuminati.png     - Illuminati confirmed triangle
- mlg.png            - MLG glasses/montage parody stuff
- troll_face.png     - U mad bro?
- nyan_cat.png       - Rainbow cat
- rick_roll.png      - Never gonna give you up

UNDANK MEMES (don't upvote or die):
- anime1.png         - Any anime meme
- anime2.png         - Another anime meme
- dead_meme.png      - Something cringe/dead

UI ELEMENTS:
- upvote_button.png  - Reddit-style upvote arrow (optional)
- downvote_button.png - Reddit-style downvote arrow (optional)
- panel_bg.png       - Background for the minigame popup (optional)

Notes:
- Meme images should be roughly 400x400 or similar square-ish
- Add more memes by editing meme_minigame.gd's meme_database array
- The more variety, the better!

To add new memes in code:
  meme_database.append({
    "path": "res://Graphics/Minigames/MemeGame/your_meme.png",
    "type": MemeType.DANK,  # or MemeType.UNDANK
    "name": "Meme Name"
  })
